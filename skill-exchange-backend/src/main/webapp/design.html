The matching model

Three signals, one weighted score.

Every mentor–learner pair is scored against skill fit, free time, and travel logistics before a match is ever shown.

50%

Skill overlap

What a learner wants is checked against what a mentor offers, filtered by proficiency level.

30%

Time compatibility

Only pairs with genuinely overlapping weekend availability move to the next round.

20%

Location logistics

Pairs are grouped by home campus, then handed to the rotation system below.
